Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 28, 2025

SubSnap ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how SubSnap handles your information when you use our iOS app.

1. Information We Collect

Screenshots & Images

When you use SubSnap to capture a screenshot, the image is processed entirely on your device. Screenshots are never uploaded to our servers. All OCR and text recognition happens locally using on-device machine learning.

Subscription Data

Any subscription information you manually enter or that is extracted from your screenshots is stored exclusively on your device using local storage. This data never leaves your phone.

App Usage Data

We do not collect any analytics, crash reports, or usage data from the SubSnap app. There is no third-party analytics SDK embedded in this app.

Device Information

We do not collect device identifiers, IP addresses, or any other device-related information.

2. How We Use Information

Since all data is stored locally on your device and never leaves it, we do not have access to, and therefore cannot use, your personal information in any way.

3. Data Storage & Security

All subscription data is stored in the local storage of your iOS device, protected by the security mechanisms built into iOS. We are unable to access your data. You are responsible for the security of your device and any local backups you create.

4. Third-Party Services

SubSnap does not integrate with any third-party services, advertising networks, or data brokers. The app contains no trackers, no ads, and no analytics.

5. Children's Privacy

SubSnap is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective date" at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
